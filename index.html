    Know your token. Know your rewards.

    You hold $BTC ("Big Titty Coin"), a community token on Solana. It is a separate token from Bitcoin. Always verify the full contract address before trading.

    The $BTC market is paired with BTC. Part of the trading fees is distributed to eligible holders as BTC rewards. You keep your $BTC position while distributions arrive in your Solana wallet. Rewards depend on trading activity and the project’s distribution rules; amounts and timing are variable.

    Rewards are Wrapped BTC (Portal / Wormhole) on Solana, a tokenized representation of Bitcoin with 8 decimal places. They are not native Bitcoin-network transfers. The verified reward mint is linked below; your wallet may label it WBTC.
